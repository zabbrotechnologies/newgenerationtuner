Our Story

Built on a
singular obsession
with paint.

New Generation Tuners was founded in 2014 with a single purpose: to deliver detailing results that most commercial garages simply cannot achieve — because they don't have the patience, the equipment or the discipline.

In ten years we have worked on everything from daily-driven hatchbacks to concours-presented European performance cars. The approach is identical regardless of vehicle value — inspect first, work methodically, never cut corners.

Every product we use is selected for a reason. Every technique is tested. Every result is verified under inspection lighting before the vehicle leaves the studio.

What We Stand For

Precision Is the Difference

01
Inspect Before You Touch

Every vehicle is assessed under raking light and measured with a paint thickness gauge before any product is selected or any pad is mounted. We understand what we are working with before we begin.

02
No Shortcuts

A proper decontamination before correction. A proper correction before coating. A proper cure before delivery. Each step matters. Skipping any of them compromises the result of every step that follows.

03
Honest Advice

If a vehicle needs a two-stage correction, we say so. If a single stage will suffice, we recommend that instead. We will never sell a service the vehicle doesn't require — or undersell a service it genuinely needs.

04
Verified Results

Every vehicle is inspected under multiple light sources before handover. We do not sign off a job unless every panel meets our standard. The result is documented. The client sees exactly what was achieved.

05
Ongoing Support

Our relationship with a vehicle doesn't end at delivery. We provide care instructions, follow-up checks, and maintenance services to ensure protection stays effective long after the initial treatment.

06
Continuous Learning

The chemistry, the equipment and the techniques in professional detailing continue to evolve. We invest in training, testing and the best available tools to ensure our methods remain current and effective.
