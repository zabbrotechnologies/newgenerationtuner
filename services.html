Educational Blueprint

YOUR PAINT TELLS A STORY.

Factory clear coat measures only 35 to 50 microns thick — thinner than a Post-it note. Every improper wash damages this irreplaceable barrier. Here is how we engineer it back to perfection:

01 · DETECT

Contamination

Brake dust, road pitch, and acid rain bond into the clear coat, creating a rough, cloudy texture that inhibits reflection.

02 · INSPECT

Surface Defects

Microscopic circular scratches (swirls) scatter light in random directions, reducing paint depth and color richness.

03 · LEVEL

Correction

Precision abrasive compounds level the highest ridges of the defect without needlessly compromising clear coat thickness.

04 · JEWEL

Refinement

Finishing micro-pads eliminate any remaining haze, achieving laser-sharp gloss and mirrored specular highlights.

05 · LOCK

Protection

SiO₂ ceramic coating or self-healing PPF seals the corrected substrate against future chemical and mechanical degradation.

Our Operating Standard

THE STANDARD OF OUR WORK

Six methodical phases. We treat every vehicle as a unique project — never a production line.

01 · ASSESS

Electronic Paint Audit

Ultrasonic gauge measurements across every panel to calculate clear coat depth and log defect topography.

02 · PREPARE

Chemical Decontamination

Multi-stage foam wash, iron fall-out dissolution, clay decontamination, and sensitive rubber trim masking.

03 · CORRECT

Micro-Level Polishing

Systematic machine correction eliminating swirl marks, water etching, and holograms under specialized high-CRI lighting.

04 · REFINE

Jewelling & Isopropyl Wipe

Ultra-fine finishing jewel pass followed by alcohol wipe-downs to inspect true unmasked surface clarity.

05 · PROTECT

Molecular Barrier Application

SiO₂ ceramic coating or computer-cut PPF installed in a temperature and humidity controlled studio bay.

06 · INSPECT

Infrared Cure & Handover

Controlled infrared lamp curing, 50-point quality verification, and client care briefing with warranty certificate.
